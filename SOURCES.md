## What this means for "instant" alerts

* Nothing can be alerted before the City publishes it. The fastest public signals are the Article 80 FeatureServer
  (edited during the business day, checked every 5 min via `lastEditDate`) and the Records Library (checked every
  10 min). The CKAN datasets (permits, applications, ZBA) are published as **daily batches**; IceCap checks their
  `last_modified` every 5 minutes around the learned publication hour and backs off to hourly otherwise.
* Records Library and Article 80 dates are **dates without times**. Publication-to-detection latency is therefore
  measured from the provider's own update timestamp where one exists (`lastEditDate`, CKAN `last_modified`), and
  shown separately from filing-date-to-detection latency (which includes the City's own publication delay).

## Network / terms notes

* All sources are official City of Boston / Boston Planning Department public endpoints. The CKAN portal
  (data.boston.gov) publishes under open data terms; ArcGIS REST services allow anonymous query.
* `robots.txt` on www.bostonplans.org disallows only staff/calendar/CMS paths; apps.bostonplans.org and
  sire.bostonplans.org expose no robots restrictions on the API paths used.
* Per-host rate limits: data.boston.gov and ArcGIS ≤ 2 req/s, 2 concurrent; sire.bostonplans.org ≤ 1 req/s, 1
  concurrent. Retries use exponential backoff with jitter and honour `Retry-After`; repeated failures open a
  per-source circuit breaker.
* In a restricted network environment the following hosts must be allowed: `gis.bostonplans.org`,
  `gisportal.boston.gov`, `data.boston.gov`, `sire.bostonplans.org`, `apps.bostonplans.org`, and (for alerts)
  `api.telegram.org` plus your SMTP host.
